# Privacy Policy for ReaderMC

**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ReaderMC ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner.

## 1. Information We Collect

ReaderMC is designed to function primarily as a local comic and manga reader. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we intentionally collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, to provide certain features and ensure the App functions correctly, we may collect and use certain non-personal information, as well as utilize third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

### 1.1 Local Device Storage
The App requires permissions to access your device's local storage (including `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` and `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`). This permission is strictly necessary to allow the App to locate, read, and display the comic and manga files (such as ZIP, CBZ, RAR, CBR, or PDF) stored on your device. 

**Important:** We **do not** upload, transmit, or share your local files, reading history, or personal documents to any external servers. All file processing is done locally on your device.

### 1.2 Device Permissions
- **Set Wallpaper:** The App requests permission to set your device's wallpaper to allow you to easily use images from your comics as your background.
- **Internet:** The App requires internet access to serve advertisements, collect anonymous analytics, and send crash reports to help us improve the App.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services collect data according to their own privacy policies.

We use the following third-party services:

*   **Google AdMob:** We use AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect and use data (such as your device's advertising ID) to show you personalized ads based on your interests.
*   **Google Analytics for Firebase:** We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App. This service collects anonymous usage data, such as the features you use and the duration of your sessions, to help us improve user experience.
*   **Firebase Crashlytics:** We use Crashlytics to monitor the App's stability. If the App crashes, Crashlytics automatically collects anonymous crash reports, including details about your device state at the time of the crash. This helps us identify and fix bugs quickly.

For more information on how Google handles your data, please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. How We Use Your Information

The limited information collected through third-party services is used for the following purposes:
*   To provide, maintain, and improve the App.
*   To serve relevant advertisements to support the development of the App.
*   To monitor and analyze usage trends and activity.
*   To detect, prevent, and resolve technical issues and crashes.

## 4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security. 

## 5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to take the necessary actions.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
